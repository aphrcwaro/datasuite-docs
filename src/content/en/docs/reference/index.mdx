# Quick reference

The [Countdown methodology](/en/docs/framework) is the method: each step of the analysis, how it is done and how to read the results. This section is for looking things up -- which page covers a step, where it is in the apps, what a term or an indicator means.

## Where each step is

| Step | Method | RMNCAH app | Vaxx app | What it produces |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Analysis setup | [Analysis setup](/en/docs/framework/1-analysis-setup) | [Getting started](/en/apps/rmncah/getting-started) | [Getting started](/en/apps/vaxx/getting-started) | The loaded dataset and its national survey rates |
| Data quality | [Data quality assessment](/en/docs/framework/2-data-quality-assessment) | [Data quality](/en/apps/rmncah/data-quality) | [Data quality](/en/apps/vaxx/data-quality) | Reporting rates, outliers, internal consistency and an overall quality score |
| Data adjustment | [Data adjustment](/en/docs/framework/3-data-adjustment) | [Data preparation](/en/apps/rmncah/data-preparation) | [Data preparation](/en/apps/vaxx/data-preparation) | Adjusted numerators, with a before-and-after comparison |
| Denominators | [Denominator assessment and selection](/en/docs/framework/4-denominator-selection) | [Denominators](/en/apps/rmncah/denominators) | [Denominators](/en/apps/vaxx/denominators) | The best-performing denominator for each indicator |
| Coverage | [National coverage](/en/docs/framework/5-coverage-estimation) | [Coverage, equity and targets](/en/apps/rmncah/coverage-equity-targets), [Advanced analysis](/en/apps/rmncah/advanced-analysis) | [Coverage, equity and targets](/en/apps/vaxx/coverage-equity-targets) | National coverage levels and trends, compared with surveys and targets |
| Bayesian coverage | [Bayesian modeling approach](/en/docs/framework/5.1-bayesian-coverage) | [Advanced analysis](/en/apps/rmncah/advanced-analysis) | -- | Modelled national and subnational coverage |
| Equity | [Equity assessment (equiplots)](/en/docs/framework/6-equity-analysis) | [Coverage, equity and targets](/en/apps/rmncah/coverage-equity-targets) | [Coverage, equity and targets](/en/apps/vaxx/coverage-equity-targets) | Inequality by residence, wealth and maternal education (equiplots) |
| Subnational | [Subnational analysis](/en/docs/framework/7-subnational-analysis) | [Sub-national analysis](/en/apps/rmncah/subnational) | [Sub-national analysis](/en/apps/vaxx/subnational) | Regional and district coverage, inequality and target attainment |
| Mortality | [Mortality](/en/docs/framework/9-mortality) | [Mortality](/en/apps/rmncah/mortality) | -- | Institutional mortality and the completeness of death reporting |
| Service utilisation | [Curative health services utilization for sick children](/en/docs/framework/10-service-utilisation) | [Service utilization and health system](/en/apps/rmncah/service-utilization-health-system) | -- | Use of curative services for sick children |
| Health system performance | [Health system performance](/en/docs/framework/11-health-system-performance) | [Service utilization and health system](/en/apps/rmncah/service-utilization-health-system) | -- | Health system inputs and outputs, and the private sector share |
| Dissemination | [Planning for dissemination, decisions and action](/en/docs/framework/12-dissemination) | [Reports](/en/apps/rmncah/reports), [Exports, reports and AI](/en/apps/rmncah/exports-and-ai) | [Reports](/en/apps/vaxx/reports), [Exports, reports and AI](/en/apps/vaxx/exports-and-ai) | Reports for review meetings and planning |
