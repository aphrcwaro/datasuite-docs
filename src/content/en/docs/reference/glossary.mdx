# Glossary

Terms as the DataSuite documentation defines them, each linked to the section it comes from. Where a page
and the methodology word a term differently, the methodology page applies.

## A

### Adjusted numerator (N_adj)

N_adj = N_rep x [1 + (1 - C) x k], where N_rep is the raw reported number and C the reporting completeness (proportion of expected reports received) ([source](/en/docs/framework/3-data-adjustment#numerator-adjustment-formulas-and-parameters))

### Admin data

District, region, country; hospitals, health centres, all facilities, private for-profit and non-profit facilities; physicians, nurses/midwives, clinical officers, non-clinician physicians; hospital beds ([source](/en/docs/reference/indicator-definitions#admin-data))

## B

### Bottom inequality (marginalization)

The poorest are left behind ([source](/en/docs/framework/6-equity-analysis#rationale-and-approach))

## C

### Completeness of facility death reporting

Completeness of facility reporting = reported iMMR / expected iMMR ([source](/en/docs/framework/9-mortality#computing-reporting-completeness))

### Consistent district

Its yearly ratio lies between 1 and 1.5 ([source](/en/apps/rmncah/data-quality#internal-consistency))

### Core health professionals per 10,000 (physicians, non-physician clinicians, nurses, midwives)

Thresholds 23 (2006 WHO) and 44.5 (UHC) ([source](/en/docs/framework/11-health-system-performance#core-health-professionals-per-10000-population))

## D

### Data checks

Per district, whether it is missing from, and how often it occurs in, each sheet ([source](/en/docs/reference/indicator-definitions#data-checks))

### Data quality indicators 1a, 1b, 1c

- 1a: monthly facility reports received / total expected reports (12 x number of facilities) ([source](/en/docs/framework/2-data-quality-assessment#reporting-completeness))
- 1b: districts with at least 90% reporting completeness / total districts ([source](/en/docs/framework/2-data-quality-assessment#reporting-completeness))
- 1c: districts with no missing values across all forms / total districts ([source](/en/docs/framework/2-data-quality-assessment#reporting-completeness))

### Data quality indicators 2a, 2b

- 2a: monthly values not classified as outliers / total monthly values; 2b: districts with no outliers / total districts ([source](/en/docs/framework/2-data-quality-assessment#outlier-detection))

### Data quality indicators 3a-3d

- 3a: ANC1 reported / Penta1 reported (expected ratio range); 3b: Penta1 reported / Penta3 reported (based on survey data); 3c and 3d: districts within the expected ratio or range / total districts ([source](/en/docs/framework/2-data-quality-assessment#internal-consistency))

### Demand satisfied (family planning)

Family planning demand satisfied = use of modern contraceptives / (unmet need + use of modern contraceptives) ([source](/en/docs/framework/5-coverage-estimation#family-planning))

### Dropout

Children who received the first dose minus those who received the second, divided by the first, as a percentage ([source](/en/apps/vaxx/coverage-equity-targets#indicators-available))

## E

### Expected institutional maternal mortality ratio (expected iMMR)

Expected iMMR = population MMR / (P_i + (M_c/M_i) x (1 - P_i)), with P_i the share of live births in facilities ([source](/en/docs/framework/9-mortality#calculating-expected-institutional-mortality))

### Extreme outlier

A monthly value 5 times the median absolute deviation (MAD) from the monthly median value for that year ([source](/en/docs/framework/2-data-quality-assessment#outlier-detection))

Also defined as: A monthly value more than 5 times the median absolute deviation (MAD) from the monthly median for that year ([source](/en/apps/rmncah/data-quality#outlier-detection))

Also defined as: A value more than 5 median absolute deviations (MAD) from the median (Hampel method) ([source](/en/apps/vaxx/data-quality#outlier-detection))

## H

### Health facilities per 10,000 (all tiers, public and private)

Below 2 indicates low infrastructure access ([source](/en/docs/framework/11-health-system-performance#number-of-health-facilities-per-10000-population))

### Hospitals per 100,000 population

 ([source](/en/docs/framework/11-health-system-performance#number-of-hospitals-per-100000-population))

## I

### Immunization denominator (BCG, Penta)

Live births - neonatal deaths ([source](/en/docs/framework/5-coverage-estimation#immunization))

### Inpatient beds per 10,000 population

 ([source](/en/docs/framework/11-health-system-performance#inpatient-bed-density-per-10000-population))

### Institutional mortality (iMMR, iSBR, neonatal deaths before discharge)

IMMR: maternal deaths in facilities per 100,000 facility live births; iSBR: stillbirths per 1,000 facility births; neonatal deaths before discharge per 1,000 facility live births ([source](/en/docs/framework/9-mortality#institutional-mortality-in-the-cd2030-analytical-approach))

## K

### k (k-factor)

The ratio of the volume of services provided by non-reporting facilities to that of reporting facilities ([source](/en/docs/framework/3-data-adjustment#approach-description-of-analytical-steps))

Also defined as: The volume of services in non-reporting facilities relative to reporting ones; 0 assumes they delivered no services, 1 the same volume ([source](/en/apps/rmncah/data-preparation#choose-the-k-factors))

Also defined as: The assumed service volume of non-reporting facilities relative to reporting ones ([source](/en/apps/vaxx/data-preparation#data-adjustment))

## L

### Linear inequality

Equidistant differences; coverage rises linearly with household wealth ([source](/en/docs/framework/6-equity-analysis#rationale-and-approach))

## M

### MADM

The median absolute deviation from the median of district coverage within an Admin1 unit, compared with that unit's coverage ([source](/en/docs/framework/7-subnational-analysis#rationale-and-approach-1))

### Measles denominators

Measles 1 denominator: live births - neonatal deaths - postneonatal deaths; Measles 2: live births - neonatal deaths - 2 x postneonatal deaths ([source](/en/docs/framework/5-coverage-estimation#immunization))

## O

### OPD visits per child per year

Adjusted under-5 OPD visits (new and re-visits) / DHIS2 projected under-5 population ([source](/en/docs/framework/10-service-utilisation#mean-number-of-opd-visits-per-child-per-year))

### Overall data quality score

The mean of indicators 1a, 1b, 2a, 2b, 3c and 3d, computed automatically ([source](/en/docs/framework/2-data-quality-assessment#overall-quality-score))

Also defined as: The mean of rows 1a, 1b, 2a, 2b, 3c and 3d of the score table ([source](/en/apps/rmncah/data-quality#overall-data-quality-score))

## P

### Pediatric case fatality rate

Children who die in hospital / total admissions (discharges + deaths) ([source](/en/docs/framework/10-service-utilisation#case-fatality-rate))

### Percent of subnational units above a target or threshold

How far universal coverage is reached subnationally; the goal is all units meeting the target, and higher means less inequality ([source](/en/docs/framework/7-subnational-analysis#rationale-and-approach-1))

### Population data

By district and year: growth rate, total population, under-5, under-1, projected live births and total births (census-based), women 15-49 ([source](/en/docs/reference/indicator-definitions#population-data))

## R

### Reporting completeness data

Monthly expected, received and rate for ANC, institutional delivery, vaccinations, OPD and IPD ([source](/en/docs/reference/indicator-definitions#reporting-completeness))

## S

### Service coverage

The population who received the service divided by the population who need it (the target population) ([source](/en/docs/framework/4-denominator-selection#objective))

### Service data 1

BCG, Penta1, Penta3, Measles1, Measles2; OPD total and under-5 (new and re-visits); IPD total and under-5; under-5 and all-age facility deaths ([source](/en/docs/reference/indicator-definitions#service-data-1))

### Service data 2

ANC1, first-trimester ANC, ANC4, IPT2, IPT3, IFA90, syphilis and HIV tests, SBA, institutional deliveries and live births, C-sections, low birth weight (&lt;2500 g), PNC within 48 hours, stillbirths (total, fresh, macerated), maternal and neonatal deaths ([source](/en/docs/reference/indicator-definitions#service-data-2))

### Service data 3

OPV1-3, Penta2, PCV1-3, rotavirus 1-2, IPV1-2, HPV1-2, measles cases ([source](/en/docs/reference/indicator-definitions#service-data-3))

## T

### Top inequality (mass deprivation)

The richest are well ahead of all other wealth quintiles ([source](/en/docs/framework/6-equity-analysis#rationale-and-approach))

### Trajectory

One possible path of coverage over time; each year's estimate is the middle value across trajectories, with a range leaving out the most extreme ones ([source](/en/docs/framework/5.1-bayesian-coverage#producing-estimates-and-short-term-forecasts))

## Z

### Zero-dose and under-vaccinated

Zero-dose and Under-vaccinated are derived from Penta1 and Penta3 ([source](/en/apps/vaxx/coverage-equity-targets#indicators-available))
