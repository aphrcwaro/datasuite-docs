# Countdown to 2030 DataSuite

**CD2030 DataSuite** is the dedicated data analytics platform for the **Countdown to 2030** initiative. It is designed to empower country and regional health data users (data analysts, researchers, and public health professionals) to track progress in women's, children's, and adolescents' health outcomes.

<Bleed>

![DataSuite welcome page](/images/datasuite.jpg)

</Bleed>

Through the embedded complementary applications ([DHIS2 Data Extractor](/en/apps/data-extractor), [Vaxx App](/en/apps/countdown) and the [RMNCAH App](/en/apps/countdown)), CD2030 DataSuite provides users with a one-stop platform for:

- Extraction of RMNCAH indicators from DHIS2, UN surveys, and other EPI program datasets
- Quality assessment and adjustment of the data
- Analysis of the data for various metrics on coverage of MNCH interventions

By seamlessly bridging the gap between routine health facility data (HMIS/DHIS2) and population survey estimates, CD2030 DataSuite provides a robust, standardized environment for complex statistical analysis that allows users to:

- Confidently assess data quality,
- Adjust for reporting gaps,
- Calculate precise/valid coverage denominators, and
- Generate actionable insights at both national and sub-national levels.
