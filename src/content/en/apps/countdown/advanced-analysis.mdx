# Advanced analysis

<Callout type="info">
This page applies to the **RMNCAH app** only.
</Callout>

Three analyses go beyond the basic coverage charts: **Bayesian Analysis**, **Continuum of Care** and **FPET FP Projection**. They build on the adjusted data and the denominators you selected, so finish [Data preparation](/en/apps/countdown/data-preparation) and [Denominators](/en/apps/countdown/denominators) first. Methods: [Bayesian modeling approach](/en/docs/methodology/bayesian-coverage) and [National coverage](/en/docs/methodology/coverage).

## Bayesian Analysis

**Purpose.** Combine survey data and routine facility data into one estimate per year, with a range of uncertainty and a short-term forecast. How the model uses each source: [Bayesian modeling approach > How are survey data used?](/en/docs/methodology/bayesian-coverage#how-are-survey-data-used) and [How are routine data used?](/en/docs/methodology/bayesian-coverage#how-are-routine-data-used)

**When to use it.** See [Bayesian modeling approach > Why it matters](/en/docs/methodology/bayesian-coverage#why-it-matters).

There are two entries in the **Bayesian Analysis** group of the sidebar: **National Analysis** and **Sub-National Analysis** (regions).

<Steps>

### Open Bayesian Analysis

Choose National Analysis or Sub-National Analysis.

### Pick an indicator tab

**ANC 4 Visits**, **ANC 1st Trimester**, **Institutional Deliveries**, **Measles 1** or **Pentavalent 3**. The model is defined for these five indicators.

### Read the chart

The title is *Bayesian Coverage Model Estimates* followed by the indicator, and the source note reads *Survey Data & DHIS2 Routine Data*. The central line is the best estimate; the band is the uncertainty range. Use **Download Plot** and **Download Data** in the toolbar.

</Steps>

![Bayesian ANC4 estimates at national level](/images/bayesian-ANC4-national.png)

![Bayesian ANC4 estimates by region](/images/bayesian-ANC4-subnational.png)

**How to interpret.** See [Bayesian modeling approach > Reading the estimates](/en/docs/methodology/bayesian-coverage#reading-the-estimates) and the [illustrative examples](/en/docs/methodology/bayesian-coverage#illustrative-examples).

## Continuum of Care

**Purpose.** Follow coverage along the care pathway and see where women and children drop out.

**Where.** **National Analysis**, **Continuum of Care** in the sidebar. The page has two cards, both titled *National Coverage & Continuum*, each with the tabs **Maternal Continuum** and **Child Continuum**.

- **Top card, continuum profile.** *National continuum-of-care profile*: the latest facility value, the latest survey value and, where relevant, the WUENIC value for each step of the selected pathway, for example ANC 1st Trimester, ANC 4 Visits, Institutional Deliveries and PNC within 48 hours for the maternal continuum, or Pentavalent 3 and Measles 1 for the child continuum.
- **Bottom card, continuum heatmap.** *Subnational continuum heatmap*: the same steps for each Admin 1 region. Read across a row to see one region, down a column to see one indicator. Red cells are low coverage, yellow intermediate and green high.

The page can generate the national coverage report with **Generate Report**.

![Continuum of care profile](/images/national-coverage-continuum-care-profile.png)

![Maternal continuum heatmap by region](/images/national-admin1-maternal-continuum-heatmap.png)

**How to interpret.** See [National coverage > Interpreting the maternal continuum](/en/docs/methodology/coverage#interpretation-1), [Interpreting the child continuum](/en/docs/methodology/coverage#interpretation-2) and [Continuum of care at subnational level](/en/docs/methodology/coverage#continuum-of-care-at-subnational-level).

## FPET FP Projection

**Purpose.** Show family planning trends for currently married women aged 15 to 49, from the Family Planning Estimation Tool (FPET, Track20).

**Where.** It is the **FP Projection** tab on **National Coverage** and on **Sub-National Coverage**.

The chart title is *Family planning among currently married women 15-49 years*. It draws two indicators: **Prevalence of Modern Methods (mCPR)** and **Demand Satisfied with a Modern Method**.

![FPET projection](/images/national-coverage-fpet.png)

**How to interpret.** See [National coverage > Family planning projections](/en/docs/methodology/coverage#family-planning-projections); where the data come from: [Family planning](/en/docs/methodology/coverage#family-planning).
