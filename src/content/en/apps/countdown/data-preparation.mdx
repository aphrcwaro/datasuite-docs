# Data preparation

Data preparation turns the reported numbers into an analysis-ready dataset, the dataset used for coverage. It has three pages: **Remove Years**, **Data Adjustment** and **Data Adjustment Changes** (in the RMNCAH app they are in the **Data quality** section). The method, formulas and business rules are in [Data adjustment](/en/docs/methodology/data-adjustment).

<Callout type="important">
Adjusting the data is required. Running the adjustment is what unlocks the later pages: in the RMNCAH app the Denominators and Analysis pages; in the Vaxx app denominators, coverage, inequality, targets and Reports stay unavailable until you run **Adjust Data** at least once.
</Callout>

## Remove Years

**Purpose.** Leave out years whose data cannot be relied on.

**When to use it.** When to remove a year, and when to keep it: [Data adjustment > Remove years](/en/docs/methodology/data-adjustment#remove-years).

<Steps>

### Open Remove Years

In the RMNCAH app it is under **Data quality** in the sidebar. Decide from the [data quality](/en/apps/countdown/data-quality) pages first.

### Select Years to Remove

In **Select Years to Remove**, the chips list the years present in the data. Select one or more.

### Press Confirm Removal

The message changes from *No years have been removed yet* to *Data for the following years has been removed*, followed by the years. To bring a year back (restore years), deselect it or clear the selection and confirm again.

</Steps>

![Remove years](/images/dqa-remove-year.png)

Removal happens before adjustment. If you have already adjusted, run **Adjust Data** again after changing the removed years.

## Data Adjustment

**Purpose.** Correct for incomplete reporting (missing reports) and extreme outliers. How missing values, outliers and the K-factor are handled: [Data adjustment > Method](/en/docs/methodology/data-adjustment#method).

The page has two cards: **K-Factors (Adjustment Factors)** and the adjustment card with **Adjust Data**, which applies the corrections to the current dataset.

<Steps>

### Choose the K-Factors

<Tabs items={['RMNCAH', 'Vaxx']} storageKey="countdown-app">
<Tabs.Tab>

The card **K-Factors (Adjustment Factors)** has four selectors: **ANC K-Factor**, **Delivery K-Factor**, **Vaccines K-Factor** and **OPD K-Factor**. Each offers 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1. What each value assumes and how to choose: [Data adjustment > Configuring the k-factor](/en/docs/methodology/data-adjustment#configuring-the-k-factor).

</Tabs.Tab>
<Tabs.Tab>

The card **K-Factors (Adjustment Factors)** has an **ANC K-Factor**, a **Delivery K-Factor** and a **Vaccines K-Factor**, each a choice of 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 or 1. The page shows the values stored in the dataset. Pick a value for each of the three factors; the starting value, the guidebook's default and how to choose: [Data adjustment > Configuring the k-factor](/en/docs/methodology/data-adjustment#configuring-the-k-factor).

</Tabs.Tab>
</Tabs>

### Press Adjust Data

<Tabs items={['RMNCAH', 'Vaxx']} storageKey="countdown-app">
<Tabs.Tab>

The message goes from *Dataset not adjusted* to *Adjusting ...* and then *Data Adjusted*. Do this even if you keep every factor at 0, because it is what unlocks the analysis pages.

</Tabs.Tab>
<Tabs.Tab>

Choose **Adjust Data** and wait for "Data Adjusted". The status box shows "Not adjusted yet", then "Adjusting ...", then "Data Adjusted".

</Tabs.Tab>
</Tabs>

### Download the result if you need it

Once adjusted, **Download Adjusted Dataset** saves the adjusted master dataset as a `.dta` file (`master_adj_dataset.dta`).

### Review the changes

Open **Data Adjustment Changes**.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
Changing the K-factors or the removed years discards the adjusted dataset. Press **Adjust Data** again, otherwise the analysis pages will not reflect your change. If the adjustment fails, the message shows the reason.
</Callout>

How a district-month with reporting below 75% (or missing) is handled: [Data adjustment > Critical data threshold rules](/en/docs/methodology/data-adjustment/#critical-data-threshold-rules).

![K-factor selection](/images/adjustment-kfactor.png)

## Data Adjustment Changes

**Purpose.** See what the adjustment did to each indicator.

**How to read it.** The card **Visualize effects of changes** has one tab per indicator. Each chart compares the number of events (of the indicator) *before* and *after* adjustment for completeness and outliers, by year.

<Tabs items={['RMNCAH', 'Vaxx']} storageKey="countdown-app">
<Tabs.Tab>

Tabs are Institutional Deliveries, Institutional Live Births, Pentavalent 1, ANC 1 and OPD Under 5 Years, plus a **Custom** tab for any other indicator. Use **Download Plot** and **Download Data** on the toolbar. **Generate Report** opens the adjustment report.

</Tabs.Tab>
<Tabs.Tab>

Tabs are **Institutional Live Births**, **BCG**, **Pentavalent 1** and **Measles 1**, plus a **Custom Check** tab with an indicator selector.

If the data have not been adjusted, the chart uses a K-factor of 0 for every service, so it shows only what outlier and missing value handling do.

</Tabs.Tab>
</Tabs>

![Before and after adjustment](/images/adjustment-before-after.png)

## How to interpret the results

How to read the before-and-after charts: [Data adjustment > Adjustment outputs](/en/docs/methodology/data-adjustment#adjustment-outputs). What to report: [Data adjustment > Report](/en/docs/methodology/data-adjustment#report).

## Common pitfalls

- Changing K-factors, removed years or the performance threshold affects every downstream page. Re-run **Adjust Data** and re-check denominators afterwards.
- Reports and pooled caches should be built from adjusted data. See **[Pooled App](/en/apps/pooled)**.

Pitfalls in choosing the K-factors and removing years, and which indicators are not adjusted: [Data adjustment > Common pitfalls](/en/docs/methodology/data-adjustment#common-pitfalls).
