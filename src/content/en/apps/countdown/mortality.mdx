# Mortality

<Callout type="info">
This page applies to the **RMNCAH app** only.
</Callout>

The **Mortality** group looks at deaths recorded in health facilities: institutional maternal mortality, stillbirths and neonatal deaths before discharge. It has three pages: **Institutional Mortality**, **Mortality Mapping** and **Mortality Completeness**. The method, definitions and formulas are in [Mortality](/en/docs/methodology/mortality).

<Callout type="warning">
Mortality figures use the reported numbers of deaths without the reporting-rate and outlier adjustment applied to other indicators (why: [Mortality > iMMR and iSBR review](/en/docs/methodology/mortality#immr-and-isbr-review)). They still depend on the denominator you selected (live births or births). If a value looks like a data entry error, check it in [Outlier Detection](/en/apps/countdown/data-quality) and treat it with the methodology's guidance ([Outlier detection thresholds](/en/docs/methodology/mortality#outlier-detection-thresholds)).
</Callout>

## Institutional Mortality

**Purpose.** Show the level and trend of institutional mortality and check whether it is plausible.

**When to use it.** First, to see the rates; then use the second card to test data quality.

<Steps>

### Open Institutional Mortality

It is under **Mortality** in the **Analysis** group.

### Read the first card, Institutional Mortality

Tabs: **Maternal Mortality** (per 100,000 live births in facilities), **Still Birth Rate** and **Neonatal Deaths**. Dots are regions and the line is the country, by year.

How to read it: [Mortality > Institutional mortality levels](/en/docs/methodology/mortality#data-interpretation-considerations).

### Read the second card, Institutional Mortality DQA

Tabs: **Fresh Stillbirths** (fresh stillbirths as a percentage of all stillbirths), **SBR to Maternal Death Ratio** (stillbirths per maternal death) and **Neonatal / Maternal Deaths** (institutional neonatal deaths per maternal death). Each chart shows the **Median** and an **Expected range**, and a note under the chart gives the range.

The ranges and how to read them: [Mortality > Expected ranges on the DQA charts](/en/docs/methodology/mortality#expected-ranges-on-the-dqa-charts) and [Reading the DQA checks](/en/docs/methodology/mortality#reading-the-dqa-checks).

</Steps>

![Institutional maternal mortality by region and year](/images/mortality-institutional-mortality.png)

![Fresh stillbirths as a percentage of total stillbirths](/images/mortality-institutional-freshsbr.png)

**How to interpret.** See [Mortality > Reading the DQA checks](/en/docs/methodology/mortality#reading-the-dqa-checks); for neonatal deaths before discharge, [Considerations for iSBR](/en/docs/methodology/mortality#considerations-for-isbr).

## Mortality Mapping

**Purpose.** Map institutional mortality across regions.

**Filters.** **Select Years** (one or several) and **Palette** (Greens, Blues, Reds or Purples; the default is Reds).

Tabs: **Maternal Mortality** and **Still Birth Rate**. Each map shows the values by region for the selected years, which makes geographic clusters visible.

![Institutional maternal mortality by region](/images/mortality-institutional-mmr-byregion.png)

How to read the map: [Mortality > Geographic distribution](/en/docs/methodology/mortality#geographic-distribution).

## Mortality Completeness

**Purpose.** Estimate how completely facilities report deaths, by comparing the reported institutional rate with an expected rate built from UN estimates.

**When to use it.** After the DQA charts, to judge whether reported mortality can be used at all.

Tabs: **Institutional MMR** and **Institutional SBR**. Each chart draws completeness for the lower bound, median and upper bound of the UN estimate, across several assumptions about how much higher community mortality is than institutional mortality. Use **Generate Report** to open the mortality report.

How it is computed: [Mortality > Computing reporting completeness](/en/docs/methodology/mortality#computing-reporting-completeness). How to narrow the scenarios with local evidence: [Evaluation scenarios and interpretation](/en/docs/methodology/mortality#evaluation-scenarios-and-interpretation).

![Completeness of facility maternal death reporting](/images/mortality-completeness.png)

Completeness relies on the UN mortality estimates. Use the built-in country data, or upload your own in the Upload Data step of [Getting started](/en/apps/countdown/getting-started).
