# Data quality

Data quality is the first analysis stage. It answers one question before any coverage is calculated: can the facility data be trusted? Five pages, under **Data quality** in the RMNCAH app and **Data Quality Assessment** in the Vaxx app, show how complete, plausible and consistent the routine data are. Their results decide whether you remove years and how you adjust the data in [Data preparation](/en/apps/countdown/data-preparation). The method is explained in [Data quality assessment](/en/docs/methodology/data-quality).

## When to use it

Use these pages right after loading the data, and again whenever you upload a corrected file. Load Data must be finished first; these pages do not need Data Adjustment. Review them in this order: Reporting Rate, Outlier Detection, Data Missingness, Internal Consistency, Overall Data Quality Score. Then move on to [Data preparation](/en/apps/countdown/data-preparation).

## Common filters

<Tabs items={['RMNCAH', 'Vaxx']} storageKey="countdown-app">
<Tabs.Tab>

Most pages have an **Indicator** chip, an **Admin Level** chip (**Admin 1** or **Admin 2**) and, when Admin 1 is selected, a region chip with **Select All** to focus on one region. The Reporting Rate page adds a **Performance Threshold** (see [Reporting Rate](#reporting-rate)).

</Tabs.Tab>
<Tabs.Tab>

- **Indicator**: pick what to assess. Outlier, missingness and consistency pages list all Vaxx indicators (ANC1, Institutional Deliveries, Institutional Live Births, BCG, IPV 1-2, Measles 1-2, OPV 1-3, Pentavalent 1-3, PCV 1-3, Rotavirus 1-2). Reporting Rate uses three service groups: ANC, Institutional Delivery and Vaccination.
- **Admin Level**: Admin 1 (regions) or Admin 2 (districts). A region selector lets you focus on the districts of one region.
- **Performance Threshold** (Reporting Rate only): a percentage, 90 by default with quick picks of 70, 80, 90 and 95. It is saved in the dataset and reused by the Overall Data Quality Score.

</Tabs.Tab>
</Tabs>

## How to use the pages

<Steps>

### Open the page from the sidebar

The pages are under **Data quality** (RMNCAH) or **Data Quality Assessment** (Vaxx). Load Data must be finished first.

### Set the filters

Choose the indicator, the admin level and, if you want, a region, as described in [Common filters](#common-filters).

### Read the chart, then the table below it

Charts show the pattern. Tables list the districts and months behind it. Use the toolbar of a card for **Download Plot** (PNG) and **Download Data** (Excel), and the *This table only* year selector for tables.

</Steps>

## Reporting Rate

Shows how completely facilities reported, by service, year and area.

<Steps>

### Choose the service, threshold and admin level

<Tabs items={['RMNCAH', 'Vaxx']} storageKey="countdown-app">
<Tabs.Tab>

- **Indicator:** ANC, Institutional Delivery, Vaccination, OPD.
- **Performance Threshold:** default 90%; quick picks 70, 80, 90, 95.
- **Admin Level.**

</Tabs.Tab>
<Tabs.Tab>

Select ANC, Institutional Delivery or Vaccination, set the threshold, then the admin level.

</Tabs.Tab>
</Tabs>

### Read Sub-National Reporting Rate

The **Heat Map** tab shows the reporting rate of the selected service for each area and year. The **Bar Graph** tab shows the rate by year.

### Read the National Reporting Rate card and the table

The **National Reporting Rate** card (in the RMNCAH app, **District Reporting Rate in** a region when a region is chosen) shows the percentage of districts whose reporting rate is below the threshold (low reporting), one panel per service, by year. **Sub-National Units with Low Reporting** lists the districts or regions below the threshold for the selected year and service.

</Steps>

What the reporting rate measures and how to read it: [Data quality > Reporting completeness](/en/docs/methodology/data-quality#reporting-completeness) and [Reporting rates](/en/docs/methodology/data-quality#reporting-rates).

<Callout type="info">
The Performance Threshold you set here is stored in the dataset and reused by the Overall Data Quality Score and, in the RMNCAH app, the Service Utilization DQA.
</Callout>

## Outlier Detection

Finds extreme monthly values that may be data entry errors. What counts as an extreme outlier: [Data quality > Outlier detection](/en/docs/methodology/data-quality#outlier-detection).

- **Filters:** **Indicator** and **Admin Level**.
- **Indicators with Outliers** card: tabs **Heat Map** (percent of monthly values that are not outliers, by area and year), **Indicator Bar Graph** (by year and indicator) and **Region Bar Graph** (by year and region).
- **Sub-National Units with Extreme Outliers:** every flagged district and month, in a table of year, month, area, reported value, median and MAD. Pick the year with the *This table only* control.
- **Sub-National Trends:** pick a region and read the month-by-month plot for the selected year: the reported value against the median, the outlier bounds (plus or minus 5 MAD) and the outliers.

How to read it (the expected share of values free of outliers, campaigns): [Data quality > Outliers](/en/docs/methodology/data-quality#outliers).

![Outlier detection heat map](/images/dqa-outlier-detection.png)

## Data Missingness

Shows which indicators and areas have missing reports.

- **Filters:** **Indicator** (with **Select All**) and **Admin Level**.
- **Indicators with Missing Data:** tabs **Heat Map** (percent of missing reports by year and area), **Complete Indicators** (percent of districts with complete data for the indicator) and **Missing Indicators by Region** (trend in completeness by region).
- **Sub-National Units with Missing Data:** a table of the districts with gaps (incomplete data) for the selected indicator and year.

How missing values are measured and handled: [Data quality > Data missingness](/en/docs/methodology/data-quality#data-missingness).

![Data missingness heat map](/images/dqa-missingness.png)

## Internal Consistency

Checks that related indicators move together and agree over time. Two cards are shown: **Ratio Plots** and **Consistency Checks**. The expected ratios and the range a district must fall in: [Data quality > Internal consistency](/en/docs/methodology/data-quality#internal-consistency). How to read the plots: [Data quality > Consistency and ratios](/en/docs/methodology/data-quality#consistency-and-ratios).

- **Consistency Checks:** tabs **ANC1 and Penta1**, **Penta1 and Penta3** and **Custom Check** (choose an **X axis** and a **Y axis**). Each dot is a district, shown with a **Linear fit** and a **Diagonal**.

![Ratio plots](/images/dqa-internal-consistency.png)

- **Consistency Checks:** tabs **ANC1 and Penta1**, **Penta1 and Penta3**, **OPV1 and OPV3**, **IPV1 and IPV2**, and **Custom Check** where you choose the X and Y indicators. Each plot draws one point per district and year, with a linear fit and a diagonal.

## Overall Data Quality Score

One annual score across all checks: one table per year, with a region filter to see a single Admin 1 area, or the national level. How the score is computed: [Data quality > Overall quality score](/en/docs/methodology/data-quality#overall-quality-score).

<Tabs items={['RMNCAH', 'Vaxx']} storageKey="countdown-app">
<Tabs.Tab>

| Block | Rows |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Completeness | % of expected monthly facility reports (national); % of districts at or above the threshold; % of districts with no missing values for the 4 forms |
| 2. Extreme outliers | % of monthly values that are not extreme outliers; % of districts with no extreme outliers |
| 3. Consistency | Ratios ANC1 / Penta1 and Penta1 / Penta3; % of districts within the expected range |

The last row is the **Annual data quality score**. Use **Download Plot** and **Download Data**. **Generate Report** opens the data quality report.

</Tabs.Tab>
<Tabs.Tab>

The table has three blocks:

1. **Completeness of monthly facility reporting** (mean of ANC, delivery, immunization): percent of expected monthly reports nationally, and percent of districts with completeness at or above the threshold, and percent of districts with no missing values (mean for common vaccines).
2. **Extreme outliers**: percent of monthly values that are not extreme outliers, and percent of districts with no extreme outliers in the year.
3. **Consistency of annual reporting**: the three ratios and the percent of districts with ANC1/Penta1, Penta1/Penta3 and OPV1/OPV3 in the expected range.

The last row is the **Annual data quality score**. Use the two download buttons on the card to save the plot and the data.

</Tabs.Tab>
</Tabs>
