# Coverage, equity and targets

The **National Analysis** section has four pages: **National Coverage**, **Coverage Target**, and under **National Inequality** the pages **Routine Data** (inequality between regions) and **Survey Data** (the Equity Assessment). All of them use the vaccination denominator you chose in **[Denominators](/en/apps/vaxx/denominators)**, and all need adjusted data. Sub-national versions are in **[Sub-national analysis](/en/apps/vaxx/subnational)**. Methods: **[coverage](/en/docs/framework/5-coverage-estimation)** and **[equity](/en/docs/framework/6-equity-analysis)**.

## Indicators available

Each page shows a strip of tabs, one indicator per tab. Most tab strips end with a **Custom Check** tab that opens an **Indicator** selector listing every Vaxx analysis indicator:

- ANC 1, Institutional Deliveries and Institutional Live Births.
- BCG, IPV 1 and 2, Measles 1 and 2, OPV 1 to 3, Pentavalent 1 to 3, PCV 1 to 3, Rotavirus 1 and 2.
- Dropout: Penta 1 to Penta 3, Penta 1 to Measles 1, Penta 3 to Measles 1, Measles 1 to Measles 2. Dropout is the number of children who received the first dose minus those who received the second, divided by the first, as a percentage.
- **Zero-dose** and **Under-vaccinated**, derived from Penta1 and Penta3.

## National Coverage

Compares DHIS2 and survey estimates of national coverage for each indicator.

<Steps>

### Pick an indicator tab

Choose a headline indicator, or **Custom Check** for any other.

### Read the chart

The chart is titled "National ... Coverage Estimates" and plots by year the **DHIS2 estimate**, the **WUENIC estimate** and the **Survey estimate** with its **95% CI**. The caption states which denominator the DHIS2 values use.

### Use the chart tools

Each chart has a toolbar to edit labels, change view or style, download the image and download the data. See **[Exports, reports and AI](/en/apps/vaxx/exports-and-ai)**.

</Steps>

![National coverage](/images/national-coverage.png)

Interpretation: DHIS2 lines that track the survey and WUENIC points support the chosen denominator. A persistent gap, or DHIS2 above 100 percent, points to a denominator or numerator problem. Return to **[data quality](/en/apps/vaxx/data-quality)** and **[denominators](/en/apps/vaxx/denominators)**.

## National Inequality: Routine Data

Shows how national coverage differs between regions, using facility data.

- Tabs and the Custom Check tab as above. Each chart shows coverage in every Admin 1 region by year (subtitle "Subnational unit: admin 1 level") against the national line.
- **Select Years** and **Palette** (Greens, Blues, Reds, Purples) filters drive the **Sub-National Mapping** card, where each tab draws a map titled "Distribution of ... by Regions" from DHIS2 data. The map follows the tab selected in the inequality card.

Wide spread between regions means the national average hides weak areas. Investigate them in **[Sub-national analysis](/en/apps/vaxx/subnational)**.

![National inequality](/images/national-coverage-inequality.png)

## National Inequality: Survey Data (Equity Assessment)

Compares coverage across household groups from the survey files loaded in the wizard.

<Steps>

### Choose the Equity Type

**Area** (Rural, Urban), **Maternal Education** (No education, Primary, Secondary or higher) or **Wealth Quintile**.

### Choose the indicator

Tabs are **Pentavalent 3**, **Measles 1**, and the dropouts Penta 1 to Penta 3 and Penta 3 to Measles 1, plus **Custom Check**.

### Read the equiplot

The chart is titled "Inequalities in ... coverage by ..." and shows coverage for each group. Distance between the points is the inequality.

</Steps>

If a survey file was not uploaded, the built-in reference data are used. Upload the country's files in the **[Load Data](/en/apps/vaxx/getting-started)** wizard for a real assessment.

## Coverage Target

Tracks the share of sub-national units meeting global targets. On this page use **Admin Level** (Admin 1 or Admin 2). Two tabs:

| Tab | Indicators | Unit meets the target when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Vaccine** | BCG, Pentavalent 3, Measles 1 | Coverage is at least 80 percent in the region or district |
| **Dropout** | Penta 1 to Penta 3, Penta 3 to Measles 1 | Dropout is below 10 percent |

The chart title states the threshold that was applied, and the bars show the percentage of units meeting it by year and indicator. The table below the chart (**Target Rate by Region** at Admin 1, **Sub-National with coverage** for Admin 2) lists the units that reach the vaccine threshold for the indicator and year chosen in its own selectors. It can be downloaded as an Excel file.

<Callout type="warning">
A rising percentage of units meeting the target is good news only if coverage does not exceed plausible levels. Check units far above 100 percent for denominator problems. For dropout, remember that lower is better, and read the chart title for the direction.
</Callout>

![Coverage against targets](/images/national-coverage-targets.png)

## Pitfalls

- Coverage and target pages change when you change the denominator. Record which denominator a result used (the chart caption shows it).
- Equity charts come from survey files and can be several years old. Compare them with routine trends, not with a single year of DHIS2 data.
- Zero-dose and Under-vaccinated depend on the denominator even more than dose coverage does.
