# Data quality

Data quality is the first analysis stage. Five pages under **Data Quality Assessment** show how complete, plausible and consistent the routine data are. Their results decide whether you remove years and how you adjust the data. Methods are described in the **[data quality assessment](/en/docs/framework/2-data-quality-assessment)** chapter.

## When to use it

Use these pages right after loading the data, and again whenever you upload a corrected file. Review them in this order: Reporting Rate, Outlier Detection, Data Missingness, Internal Consistency, Overall Data Quality Score.

## Common filters

- **Indicator**: pick what to assess. Outlier, missingness and consistency pages list all Vaxx indicators (ANC1, Institutional Deliveries, Institutional Live Births, BCG, IPV 1-2, Measles 1-2, OPV 1-3, Pentavalent 1-3, PCV 1-3, Rotavirus 1-2). Reporting Rate uses three service groups: ANC, Institutional Delivery and Vaccination.
- **Admin Level**: Admin 1 (regions) or Admin 2 (districts). A region selector lets you focus on the districts of one region.
- **Performance Threshold** (Reporting Rate only): a percentage, 90 by default with quick picks of 70, 80, 90 and 95. It is saved in the dataset and reused by the Overall Data Quality Score.

## Reporting Rate

How completely facilities reported, by year and area.

<Steps>

### Choose the service, threshold and admin level

Select ANC, Institutional Delivery or Vaccination, set the threshold, then the admin level.

### Read Sub-National Reporting Rate

The **Heat Map** tab shows the reporting rate for each area and year. The **Bar Graph** tab shows the rate by year.

### Read the National Reporting Rate card and the table

The National Reporting Rate card shows the percentage of districts with low reporting (below the threshold) by service and year. **Sub-National Units with Low Reporting** lists the areas below the threshold for the selected year and service.

</Steps>

![Reporting rate by year](/images/dqa-reporting-rate.png)

## Outlier Detection

Finds extreme monthly values that may be data entry errors. A value is an outlier when it lies more than 5 median absolute deviations (MAD) from the median (Hampel method).

- **Indicators with Outliers** card: tabs **Heat Map** (percent of non-outlier values by area and year), **Indicator Bar Graph** (by year and indicator) and **Region Bar Graph** (by year and region).
- **Sub-National Units with Extreme Outliers**: table of year, month, area, reported value, median and MAD. Pick the year with the "This table only" control.
- **Sub-National Trends**: monthly plot for one region and the selected year, showing the reported value, the median, the outlier bounds (plus and minus 5 MAD) and the outliers.

![Outlier detection](/images/dqa-outlier-detection.png)

## Data Missingness

Shows which indicators and areas have missing data.

- Tabs **Heat Map** (missingness percentage by area and year), **Complete Indicators** (percent of districts with complete data for the indicator) and **Missing Indicators by Region** (trend in completeness by region).
- **Sub-National Units with Missing Data**: table of areas with incomplete data for the selected indicator and year. The indicator selector has a Select All option.

![Data missingness](/images/dqa-missingness.png)

## Internal Consistency

Checks that related indicators agree over time.

- **Ratio Plots**: ratios ANC1 / Penta1, Penta1 / Penta3 and OPV1 / OPV3 by year, compared with the expected ratios.
- **Consistency Checks**: tabs **ANC1 and Penta1**, **Penta1 and Penta3**, **OPV1 and OPV3**, **IPV1 and IPV2**, and **Custom Check** where you choose the X and Y indicators. Each plot draws one point per district and year, with a linear fit and a diagonal. Points far from the diagonal indicate inconsistent reporting.

![Consistency checks](/images/dqa-conistency-checks.png)

## Overall Data Quality Score

One annual score across all checks. Filter by region or use the national level. The table has three blocks:

1. **Completeness of monthly facility reporting** (mean of ANC, delivery, immunization): percent of expected monthly reports nationally, and percent of districts with completeness at or above the threshold, and percent of districts with no missing values (mean for common vaccines).
2. **Extreme outliers**: percent of monthly values that are not extreme outliers, and percent of districts with no extreme outliers in the year.
3. **Consistency of annual reporting**: the three ratios and the percent of districts with ANC1/Penta1, Penta1/Penta3 and OPV1/OPV3 in the expected range.

The last row is the **Annual data quality score**. Use the two download buttons on the card to save the plot and the data.

![Overall data quality score](/images/dqa-overall-score.png)

## Interpretation and pitfalls

<Callout type="warning">
A high national reporting rate can hide many low-reporting districts. Always check the district view and the score by region.
</Callout>

- A year with many outliers or low completeness is a candidate for **[Remove Years](/en/apps/vaxx/data-preparation)** or careful adjustment.
- Consistency plots show plausibility, not truth: an unusual Penta1/Penta3 ratio can be a real dropout problem or a data problem.
- Changing the threshold changes the score, so agree it with your team before comparing years or countries.
