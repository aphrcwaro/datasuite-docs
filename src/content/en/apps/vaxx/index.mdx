# Vaxx App overview

The Vaxx App is the DataSuite app for **immunization coverage and inequalities**. It takes a country's routine facility data (usually exported with the **[Data Extractor](/en/apps/data-extractor)**), checks and adjusts it, chooses a denominator, and then estimates coverage for routine vaccines, dropout, zero-dose and under-vaccinated children. Coverage can be compared with survey and WUENIC estimates, followed over time, split by region, district, area of residence, maternal education and wealth, and tracked against global targets.

It follows the same **[CD2030 analytical framework](/en/docs/framework/0-workflow)** as the **[RMNCAH App](/en/apps/rmncah)**, but is narrowed to immunization: the indicator list, the survey inputs, the adjustment factors and the analysis pages are all immunization-specific.

## Who it is for

- Immunization programme managers and national EPI analysts who need routine coverage estimates that are quality-checked and comparable with surveys.
- Analysts in the Countdown immunization coverage and inequalities work, at national and district level.
- Anyone who needs a Word, PDF or PowerPoint report of data quality and coverage for a country.

## What you need

| Input | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Facility data | One file per country: `.xls`, `.xlsx`, `.dta` or `.rds`. An Excel workbook needs an `Admin_data` sheet, a `Population_data` sheet, a `Reporting_completeness` sheet and at least one `Service_data` sheet. See **[Getting started](/en/apps/vaxx/getting-started)**. |
| Survey coverage values | The most recent national survey coverage for ANC1, institutional delivery, BCG, Penta1, Penta3, OPV1, OPV3 and Measles 1, plus national rates used for denominators. Typed or pre-filled in the upload wizard. |
| Survey files (optional) | National, regional, area, maternal education and wealth quintile `.dta` files, needed for the Equity Assessment and survey comparisons. |
| Shapefile (optional) | Used for maps. A standard shapefile for the country is built in. |
| DataSuite | The app runs inside DataSuite. Open it from the Start page. |

## How Vaxx differs from the RMNCAH App

| | Vaxx App | RMNCAH App |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Focus | Immunization | Reproductive, maternal, newborn, child and adolescent health |
| Indicators | ANC1, institutional deliveries and live births, BCG, IPV 1-2, Measles 1-2, OPV 1-3, Pentavalent 1-3, PCV 1-3, Rotavirus 1-2, plus derived dropout, zero-dose and under-vaccinated indicators | ANC (including ANC4, IPT, syphilis, IFA, HIV test), delivery, C-section, low birth weight, PNC, stillbirths and deaths, a small set of vaccines, outpatient and inpatient visits |
| Reporting Rate services | ANC, Institutional Delivery, Vaccination | Adds outpatient visits |
| Consistency checks | ANC1 and Penta1, Penta1 and Penta3, OPV1 and OPV3, IPV1 and IPV2, plus a custom pair | ANC1 and Penta1, Penta1 and Penta3, plus a custom pair |
| Adjustment factors | ANC, Delivery and Vaccines K-Factors | Adds an OPD K-Factor |
| Denominator | One vaccination denominator | A maternal and a vaccination denominator |
| Coverage targets | Vaccine (BCG, Penta3, Measles 1) and Dropout | ANC4, institutional live births and vaccine |
| Survey inputs in the wizard | Vaccine coverage values, ANC1 and institutional delivery | Adds ANC4, C-section, low birth weight and UN mortality estimates |
| Extra pages | None | Bayesian analysis, continuum of care, family planning, mortality, service utilization, MCH curative index, health system performance, private sector |
| Saved copy | `name_vaccine.rds` | A saved copy for its own indicator group |

<Callout type="warning">
A dataset saved by one app cannot be opened in the other. Opening an RMNCAH `.rds` in Vaxx is refused with a message that the dataset was built for a different indicator group.
</Callout>

## Navigating the app

The left navigation has five sections. Pages under Denominators, Analysis and Output become available once the data has been adjusted.

| Section | Pages | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start | Introduction, Load Data | **[Getting started](/en/apps/vaxx/getting-started)** |
| Data quality | Reporting Rate, Outlier Detection, Data Missingness, Internal Consistency, Overall Data Quality Score | **[Data quality](/en/apps/vaxx/data-quality)** |
| Data quality (preparation) | Remove Years, Data Adjustment, Data Adjustment Changes | **[Data preparation](/en/apps/vaxx/data-preparation)** |
| Denominators | Population Trend, Denominator Selection | **[Denominators](/en/apps/vaxx/denominators)** |
| Analysis, National | National Coverage, Coverage Target, National Inequality (Routine Data, Survey Data) | **[Coverage, equity and targets](/en/apps/vaxx/coverage-equity-targets)** |
| Analysis, Sub-National | Sub-National Coverage, Sub-National Inequality, Coverage Target | **[Sub-national analysis](/en/apps/vaxx/subnational)** |
| Output | Reports | **[Reports](/en/apps/vaxx/reports)**, **[Exports, reports and AI](/en/apps/vaxx/exports-and-ai)** |

Every page has a header with the page title and a Get Help button that opens the matching help chapter. Pages tied to a standard report also show a Generate Report button. The header also holds the language switch (English, French, Portuguese).

## Recommended order

1. Load the data and finish the wizard.
2. Review the five data quality pages, then decide whether to remove years.
3. Adjust the data. This unlocks the remaining pages.
4. Compare denominators and choose one.
5. Read national coverage, inequality, equity and targets, then the sub-national pages.
6. Build a report (see [Reports](/en/apps/vaxx/reports)).

Methods behind each step are described in the framework chapters: **[data quality](/en/docs/framework/2-data-quality-assessment)**, **[adjustment](/en/docs/framework/3-data-adjustment)**, **[denominators](/en/docs/framework/4-denominator-selection)**, **[coverage](/en/docs/framework/5-coverage-estimation)**, **[equity](/en/docs/framework/6-equity-analysis)** and **[sub-national analysis](/en/docs/framework/7-subnational-analysis)**. Indicator definitions are in **[Indicator definitions](/en/docs/reference/indicator-definitions)**.
