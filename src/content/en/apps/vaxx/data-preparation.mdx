# Data preparation

Data preparation turns the reported data into the dataset used for coverage. It has three pages: **Remove Years**, **Data Adjustment** and **Data Adjustment Changes**. The rules are explained in the **[data adjustment](/en/docs/framework/3-data-adjustment)** chapter.

<Callout type="important">
Adjusting the data is required. Denominators, coverage, inequality, targets and Reports stay unavailable until you run **Adjust Data** at least once.
</Callout>

## Remove Years

Use this page to leave out years whose data cannot be relied on, for example a year with almost no reporting or a known system failure.

<Steps>

### Decide from the data quality pages

Confirm with the **[data quality](/en/apps/vaxx/data-quality)** pages that a year is unusable.

### Select the years

In **Select Years to Remove**, choose one or more years.

### Confirm

Choose **Confirm Removal**. The message box then reads "Data for the following years has been removed" and lists them. To restore years, clear the selection and confirm again ("No years have been removed yet").

</Steps>

![Remove years](/images/dqa-remove-year.png)

Removal happens before adjustment. If you have already adjusted, run **Adjust Data** again after changing the removed years.

## Data Adjustment

The page has two cards.

**K-Factors (Adjustment Factors)**: an **ANC K-Factor**, a **Delivery K-Factor** and a **Vaccines K-Factor**, each a choice of 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 or 1. The K-factor is the assumed service volume of non-reporting facilities relative to reporting ones. The framework default is 0.25, and the page shows the values stored in the dataset.

**Adjust**: **Adjust Data** applies the corrections to the current dataset. The adjustment covers missing reports and extreme outliers. The status box shows "Not adjusted yet", then "Adjusting ...", then "Data Adjusted". Once adjusted, **Download Adjusted Dataset** saves `master_adj_dataset.dta`.

<Steps>

### Choose the K-factors

Pick a value for each of the three factors. Keep 0.25 unless you have evidence that non-reporting facilities carry a different share of services, for example a large private sector that reports poorly.

### Run the adjustment

Choose **Adjust Data** and wait for "Data Adjusted".

### Review the changes

Open **Data Adjustment Changes**.

</Steps>

![K-factor selection](/images/adjustment-kfactor.png)

## Data Adjustment Changes

Shows how the adjustment changed each indicator. Under **Visualize effects of changes**, tabs are **Institutional Live Births**, **BCG**, **Pentavalent 1** and **Measles 1**, plus a **Custom Check** tab with an indicator selector. Each chart compares the number of the indicator before and after adjustment for completeness and outliers.

If the data have not been adjusted, the chart uses a K-factor of 0 for every service, so it shows only what outlier and missing value handling do.

![Before and after adjustment](/images/adjustment-before-after.png)

## Interpretation and pitfalls

- Small differences between before and after are normal. A large jump in one year points to very low reporting or to an outlier that needs a second look in **[Outlier Detection](/en/apps/vaxx/data-quality)**.
- According to the framework, months or districts with reporting below 75 percent are not scaled up, so adjustment cannot repair very incomplete data. Consider removing such years.
- Changing K-factors, removed years or the performance threshold affects every downstream page. Re-run **Adjust Data** and re-check denominators afterwards.
- Reports and pooled caches should be built from adjusted data. See **[Pooled App](/en/apps/pooled)**.
