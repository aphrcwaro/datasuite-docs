# DataSuite applications

The CD2030 DataSuite includes a set of integrated applications designed to support the analysis of routine health facility data.
Each app plays a specific role within the analytical workflow, from data extraction to analysis and reporting.

| App | Use it to | Input |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Data Extractor](/en/apps/data-extractor) | Pull routine data out of DHIS2 and map it to standard indicators | A DHIS2 instance |
| [RMNCAH App](/en/apps/rmncah) | Analyse reproductive, maternal, newborn, child and adolescent health indicators end to end | `.xls`, `.xlsx`, `.dta`, `.rds` |
| [Vaxx App](/en/apps/vaxx) | Analyse immunization coverage and inequalities | `.xls`, `.xlsx`, `.dta`, `.rds` |
| [Pooled App](/en/apps/pooled) | Pool the saved analyses of several countries or datasets for regional comparison | A folder of `.rds` caches |

## [CD2030 Data Extractor](/en/apps/data-extractor)

The Data Extractor connects to DHIS2 systems and allows users to extract and map routine health facility data into standardized formats for analysis.

[Go to Data Extractor →](/en/apps/data-extractor)

## [RMNCAH App](/en/apps/rmncah)

The RMNCAH App supports analysis across reproductive, maternal, newborn, child, and adolescent health indicators, applying the standardized CD2030 analytical approach. It covers data quality, denominators, coverage, equity and sub-national analysis, and adds Bayesian analysis, continuum of care, family planning, mortality, service utilization and health system pages.

[Go to RMNCAH App →](/en/apps/rmncah)

## [Vaxx App](/en/apps/vaxx)

The Vaxx App focuses on immunization. It follows the same analytical stages as the RMNCAH App (data quality, adjustment, denominators, coverage, inequality and targets, national and sub-national) for immunization indicators.

[Go to Vaxx App →](/en/apps/vaxx)

## [Pooled App](/en/apps/pooled)

The Pooled App is used for **regional and multi-country analysis**. It loads the caches saved by the RMNCAH and Vaxx apps from a folder, lets you choose the domain (RMNCAH or Immunization), processes the selected dataset and exports the pooled result as CSV or Excel.

[Go to Pooled App →](/en/apps/pooled)
