# Data quality

The **Data quality** section answers one question before any coverage is calculated: can the facility data be trusted? It contains five pages. Read them in this order, then move on to [Data preparation](/en/apps/rmncah/data-preparation). The method is explained in [Data quality assessment](/en/docs/framework/2-data-quality-assessment).

## How to use the pages

<Steps>

### Open the page from Data quality in the sidebar

Load Data must be finished first. These pages do not need Data Adjustment.

### Set the filters

Most pages have an **Indicator** chip, an **Admin Level** chip (**Admin 1** or **Admin 2**) and, when Admin 1 is selected, a region chip with **Select All** to focus on one region.

### Read the chart, then the table below it

Charts show the pattern. Tables list the districts and months behind it. Use the toolbar of a card for **Download Plot** (PNG) and **Download Data** (Excel), and the *This table only* year selector for tables.

</Steps>

## Reporting Rate

Shows how completely facilities reported, by service, year and area.

- **Filters:** **Indicator** (ANC, Institutional Delivery, Vaccination, OPD), **Performance Threshold** (default 90%; quick picks 70, 80, 90, 95) and **Admin Level**.
- **Sub-National Reporting Rate:** tabs **Heat Map** and **Bar Graph** show the reporting rate of the selected service by year for each area.
- **National Reporting Rate** (or **District Reporting Rate in** a region): the percent of districts whose reporting rate is below the threshold, one panel per service.
- **Sub-National Units with Low Reporting:** the districts or regions under the threshold for the selected year.

<Callout type="info">
The Performance Threshold you set here is stored in the dataset and reused by the Overall Data Quality Score and the Service Utilization DQA.
</Callout>

![Sub-national reporting rate heat map](/images/dqa-reporting-rate.png)

## Outlier Detection

Finds extreme monthly values. A value is an extreme outlier when it lies more than 5 times the median absolute deviation (MAD) away from the monthly median for that year.

- **Filters:** **Indicator** and **Admin Level**.
- **Indicators with Outliers:** tabs **Heat Map**, **Indicator Bar Graph** and **Region Bar Graph** show the percent of monthly values that are not outliers.
- **Sub-National Units with Extreme Outliers:** every flagged district and month with the value, median and MAD.
- **Sub-National Trends:** pick a region and read the month-by-month line against the median and the outlier bounds (plus or minus 5 MAD).

Expect at least 99% of monthly values, and 90% of districts, to be free of extreme outliers. A sudden peak may be a campaign, not an error. Check the trend before you decide.

![Outlier detection heat map](/images/dqa-outlier-detection.png)

## Data Missingness

Shows which indicators and areas have missing reports.

- **Filters:** **Indicator** (with **Select All**) and **Admin Level**.
- **Indicators with Missing Data:** tabs **Heat Map** (percent of missing reports by year and area), **Complete Indicators** (percent of districts with complete data) and **Missing Indicators by Region** (trend in completeness).
- **Sub-National Units with Missing Data:** the districts with gaps for the selected indicator and year.

![Data missingness heat map](/images/dqa-missingness.png)

## Internal Consistency

Checks that related indicators move together. Two cards are shown.

- **Ratio Plots:** the yearly ratios ANC1 / Penta1 and Penta1 / Penta3 against the expected ratio. Expected ratios come from the survey coverage values entered in National Rates, so wrong survey values distort this check.
- **Consistency Checks:** tabs **ANC1 and Penta1**, **Penta1 and Penta3** and **Custom Check** (choose an **X axis** and a **Y axis**). Each dot is a district, shown with a **Linear fit** and a **Diagonal**. Dots far from the diagonal deserve a look.

A district counts as consistent when its yearly ratio lies between 1 and 1.5.

![Ratio plots](/images/dqa-internal-consistency.png)

![Consistency checks between ANC1 and Penta1](/images/dqa-conistency-checks.png)

## Overall Data Quality Score

One table per year, with a region filter to see a single Admin 1 area.

| Block | Rows |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Completeness | % of expected monthly facility reports (national); % of districts at or above the threshold; % of districts with no missing values for the 4 forms |
| 2. Extreme outliers | % of monthly values that are not extreme outliers; % of districts with no extreme outliers |
| 3. Consistency | Ratios ANC1 / Penta1 and Penta1 / Penta3; % of districts within the expected range |

The last row is the **Annual data quality score**, the mean of the national and district completeness rows (1a, 1b), the two outlier rows (2a, 2b) and the two district consistency rows (3c, 3d). Use **Download Plot** and **Download Data**. **Generate Report** opens the data quality report.

![Overall data quality score](/images/dqa-overall-score.png)

## Common pitfalls

- A low score is a reason to investigate, not always to discard a year. Compare with local events before using [Remove Years](/en/apps/rmncah/data-preparation).
- Reporting rate measures whether reports arrived, not whether the numbers are right. Use Outlier Detection and Internal Consistency together.
- District results are noisier than regional ones because they rest on smaller numbers.
