# Analysis steps

Analysis in the RMNCAH App follows a logical flow with the following steps. Each step names the guide that describes the pages involved; the step title links to the methodology chapter.

<Steps>

### [Loading data](/en/docs/framework/1-analysis-setup)

In the app: **Load Data**. See [Getting started](/en/apps/rmncah/getting-started).

![Loading routine data](/images/rmncah-loading-data.jpg)

### [Data quality assessment](/en/docs/framework/2-data-quality-assessment)

In the app: **Data quality** pages. See [Data quality](/en/apps/rmncah/data-quality).

![Data quality assessment overall score](/images/dqa-overall-score.png)

### [Data adjustment](/en/docs/framework/3-data-adjustment)

In the app: **Remove Years**, **Data Adjustment** and **Data Adjustment Changes**. See [Data preparation](/en/apps/rmncah/data-preparation). This step unlocks all the analysis pages.

### [Denominator selection](/en/docs/framework/4-denominator-selection)

In the app: **Denominator Assessment** and **Denominator Selection**. See [Denominators](/en/apps/rmncah/denominators).

![Best denominator selection](/images/denominator-best-selection.png)

### [Coverage estimation](/en/docs/framework/5-coverage-estimation)

In the app: **National Coverage**, **Coverage Target**, **Continuum of Care** and **Bayesian Analysis**. See [Coverage, equity and targets](/en/apps/rmncah/coverage-equity-targets) and [Advanced analysis](/en/apps/rmncah/advanced-analysis).

![National coverage estimates](/images/coverage-national.png)

### [Equity analysis](/en/docs/framework/6-equity-analysis)

In the app: **National Inequality** and **Equity Assessment**. See [Coverage, equity and targets](/en/apps/rmncah/coverage-equity-targets).

![Equity analysis by area of residence](/images/equity-area.png)

### [Sub-national analysis](/en/docs/framework/7-subnational-analysis)

In the app: **Sub-National Coverage**, **Sub-National Inequality** and **Coverage Target**. See [Sub-national analysis](/en/apps/rmncah/subnational).

![Sub-national coverage against targets](/images/subnational-coverage-targets.png)

### [Mortality analysis](/en/docs/framework/9-mortality)

In the app: **Mortality** pages. See [Mortality](/en/apps/rmncah/mortality).

![Mortality data completeness](/images/mortality-completeness.png)

### [Service utilization](/en/docs/framework/10-service-utilisation)

In the app: **Service Utilization** pages. See [Service utilization and health system](/en/apps/rmncah/service-utilization-health-system).

![Service utilization data quality assessment](/images/service-utilization-dqa.png)

### [Health system performance](/en/docs/framework/11-health-system-performance)

In the app: **Health System Performance** pages. See [Service utilization and health system](/en/apps/rmncah/service-utilization-health-system).

![Health system performance](/images/health-system.png)

### [Dissemination, decisions, and action](/en/docs/framework/12-dissemination)

In the app: **Reports** (see [Reports](/en/apps/rmncah/reports)) and the download buttons on every chart and table (see [Exports, reports and AI](/en/apps/rmncah/exports-and-ai)).

### [Leveraging AI for analysis and interpretation](/en/apps/ai-assistant)

In the app: the DataSuite chat assistant works on the same saved dataset. See [Exports, reports and AI](/en/apps/rmncah/exports-and-ai).

</Steps>
