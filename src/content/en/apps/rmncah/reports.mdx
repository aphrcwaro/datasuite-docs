# Reports

The **Reports** page in the **Output** section builds reports from the loaded dataset. There are two kinds:

| Kind | What it is | Files you can download |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Document | Pages of text, charts and tables, with an optional cover page and contents | Word (`.docx`) or PDF |
| Slide deck | Slides with text boxes, charts, tables and pictures | PowerPoint (`.pptx`) or PDF |

Charts and tables are drawn from the data every time you preview or download, so a report always reflects the current state of your analysis (years removed, adjustment, denominators). Every report you create is saved in the dataset and listed under **Your reports**. For downloading single charts and tables, datasets and the AI assistant, see [Exports, reports and AI](/en/apps/rmncah/exports-and-ai).

**Reports are stored inside the dataset file.** Creating or editing a report rewrites the saved dataset (the `.rds` cache), and the report list travels with it. Keep a copy of the dataset before you experiment, and share the dataset if you want a colleague to open your reports.

<Callout type="info">
The page subtitle in the app still says "Word and PDF reports". The code also builds slide decks, and the one-pager is a PowerPoint deck.
</Callout>

## Standard reports in the RMNCAH App

A standard report is the Countdown report of one analysis section, rebuilt as editable blocks. It opens as your own copy: change or delete anything. Text blocks written as questions (headed **Notes** or **Interpretations**) are prompts for the analyst; replace them with the interpretation agreed at your review meeting.

| Standard report | Kind | What it contains | Page with a **Generate Report** button |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Data quality** | Document | Data quality summary table, district reporting rates, ANC1/Penta1 and Penta1/Penta3 consistency, with questions on score, completeness and outliers | Overall Data Quality Score |
| **Data adjustment** | Document | Background, then reported numbers before and after adjustment for institutional live births and Penta1, with a prompt to describe the effect in the latest year | Data Adjustment Changes |
| **Denominator selection** | Document | DHIS2 and UN projections of population, live births and under 1, then coverage of Penta3 and institutional live births under each denominator, national and by region | Denominator Selection |
| **National coverage** | Document | Coverage of ANC4, ANC in the first trimester, delivery care (institutional deliveries and institutional live births), PNC 48h, low birth weight, Penta3 and Measles 1; maternal and child continuum of care; family planning (FPET); districts reaching targets; continuum by region | Continuum of Care |
| **National inequality** | Document | For institutional live births and Penta3: coverage by region over time, map, equity by wealth and education; then coverage by district | Equity Assessment |
| **Mortality** | Document | Institutional maternal (iMMR), stillbirth (iSBR) and neonatal mortality trends, stillbirths per maternal death, completeness of death reporting | Mortality Completeness |
| **Curative health services** | Document | Service utilization data quality table, outpatient and inpatient charts and maps, preventive vs curative index | MCH Preventive vs Curative Index |
| **Health system performance** | Document | Health system inputs table and by-region charts (facilities, hospitals, health workers, beds), service outputs by inputs | PHC Performance |
| **Public/private share** | Document | Ownership of facilities and the private share of services, nationally and by rural/urban area | Public/Private Share |
| **Synthesis chartbook** | Document, chartbook page (13.9 x 22 in) | All six analysis areas (data quality and denominators, coverage and targets, equity and geography, mortality, curative services, health system) with interpretation questions and a cover for the country team | Reports page only |
| **Sub-national one-pager** | Slide deck, one 16:9 slide | One region: Penta3, institutional live births and ANC4 against other regions and over time, the data quality score table and a coverage table by district | Reports page only |

Two of these are the ones most stakeholders ask for. The **synthesis chartbook** is the complete analysis in one document for the review meeting. The **one-pager** gives each region its own statistical profile.

## Open the Reports page

<Steps>

### Finish the prerequisites

Reports stays locked until you have loaded a dataset and run **Data Adjustment** (see [Data preparation](/en/apps/rmncah/data-preparation)). Choose denominators on [Denominator Selection](/en/apps/rmncah/denominators) first: most charts use them.

### Open it

Click **Reports** under **Output**, or click **Reports** in the top header. A **Generate Report** button in a page header also opens the page and starts the matching standard report.

</Steps>

## Generate a standard report

<Steps>

### Choose a report

Under **Standard reports** (documents) or **Standard slide decks**, click a card. Each card shows how many charts and tables it holds. You can also start from **New document** or **New slide deck** (**Start blank**).

### Name it and choose its language

A dialog asks for the report name (for example, the report and country), the **Report language** (English, French or Portuguese) and confirms with **Create report**. The report language is independent of the app language: the standard text is written in it, and the charts, tables and dates are always drawn in it. You can change it later on the **Layout** tab; charts and tables are redrawn, but text you wrote yourself is not translated.

### Wait for the charts

The editor opens at once. Each chart shows **Drawing from the dataset** and appears one by one. A chart that cannot be drawn shows **Could not be drawn with this dataset** with the reason.

### Set the region, if the report has one

For the one-pager (and any chart drawn for one region), pick the region on the **Layout** tab, under **Report**, in **Region of this report**. The first region in alphabetical order is chosen for you. Leave it empty for a national report. To produce a one-pager per region, duplicate the report from the list and change the region.

### Edit, then check

Replace the prompts, delete what you do not need, then open **Print layout** to see the final pages (see below).

### Download

Choose **Download**, pick the format and wait for the progress bar (**Drawing charts from the dataset**, then **Writing the Word file**, **Printing the PDF** or **Writing the PowerPoint file**). The file downloads by itself; **Download again** is offered if it does not.

</Steps>

## The document editor

- **Blocks panel** (left). The **Blocks** tab lists every chart and table kind grouped by analysis area, with a search box (**Search charts, tables, text**); **Text and layout** holds **Heading**, **Sub-heading**, **Paragraph**, **Notes box** and **Page break**. Drag a block onto a page, or press **+** to add it after the selected block. The **Outline** tab lists the report's structure. A **Custom** group is announced for charts made for your dataset; it is empty for now.
- **Chart and table settings.** Select a block to set what it shows: **Indicator**, **Level** (National, Region or District), a variant such as Maternal or Child, **Year**, **Region**, **Width** (**Full width**, **Half** or **Third**), a title and **Show caption and source**. **Customize this chart** changes text, fonts and colours for this chart in this report only. Charts you customised in the app with **Apply to: Report** keep that styling.
- **Order and removal.** Drag a block by its handle, or use **Move up**, **Move down**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**. **Undo** and **Redo** (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y) cover edits. Changes save automatically (**Saved in the dataset**).
- **Text.** Type directly. Enter starts a paragraph, Shift+Enter a new line. Insert **Fields** such as `{country}`, `{region}`, `{first_year}`, `{latest_year}`, `{chart_indicator}` or coverage values like `{penta3_latest}`; they are filled in from the data and follow the report language.
- **Design.** The **Design** tab holds themes (**Countdown**, **Ministry of Health**, **Minimal**, **Formal**), colours, fonts, the **Cover page** (title, subtitle, logos, country flag, editors, date, reference) and **Contents**. **Layout** sets page size (A4, Letter, chartbook, poster), orientation, margins, page numbers, header and footer. **Browse for Themes** builds a theme from a PowerPoint or Word file (`.potx`, `.pptx`, `.dotx`, `.docx`); downloads are then written from that file.
- **Views.** **Edit**, **Read mode**, **Print layout** (the exact final pages), **Focus** and zoom.
- **Slide decks** use a slide editor with layouts, a slide sorter, speaker notes and text boxes; charts and tables are placed on slides in the same way.

## Formats and downloads

| Format | Made by | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Word `.docx` | The app | Editable. On Windows with Word installed, Word also fills in the contents page and embeds fonts |
| PDF of a document | Made from the Word file by Microsoft Word or LibreOffice, so pages match Word exactly | If neither is installed, Chrome, Edge or Chromium prints it: close to the Word file, but pages can differ |
| PowerPoint `.pptx` | The app | Text, charts and tables stay editable in PowerPoint |
| PDF of a slide deck | PowerPoint or LibreOffice | Not possible without one of them |

The file is named after the country, the report name and the date, for example `Kenya_Synthesis_chartbook_2026-09-26.docx`, and is saved wherever your browser or DataSuite saves downloads. A document cannot be exported as PowerPoint, nor a deck as Word.

## Prerequisites that change report content

- **Removed years and adjustment** decide the numbers in every coverage, adjustment and mortality chart.
- **Denominators** chosen on Denominator Selection are used by the coverage charts. Without them, the coverage-by-denominator trend chart says **Choose the denominators first**.
- **Surveys.** Equity charts and facility-versus-survey comparisons need the survey files you loaded.
- **Country reference data.** The FPET chart needs family planning estimates for the country.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause and fix |
| --- | --- |
| **Could not be drawn with this dataset** in a block | The dataset lacks the data. Read the reason after it, load the missing file or delete the block. The same text appears in the exported file |
| **Choose a region** | A regional chart has no region: set **Region of this report** |
| **No district coverage for ... in ...** | Pick a year and region that have data |
| Final pages or PDF fail, or say a converter is missing | Install Microsoft Word or LibreOffice on the computer running the app. Word download still works |
| Slide deck PDF fails | Needs PowerPoint or LibreOffice. Download PowerPoint instead |
| Slow generation | The first draw of a long report, such as the synthesis chartbook, computes every chart. Wait for the progress bar; later edits redraw only what changed |

## Tips for stakeholders

1. Settle data quality, adjustment and denominators before you generate the synthesis, and state them in a text block on the first page.
2. Answer every interpretation prompt or delete it. Leaving prompts in is the most common reason a chartbook looks unfinished.
3. Fill in the cover: title, editors, date and logos of the partners.
4. Produce the report in the language of the audience, and use **Print layout** before you send a PDF.
5. Duplicate the one-pager for each region and share the PowerPoint files with the regional teams.
6. Link findings to actions: see [Dissemination, decisions and action](/en/docs/framework/12-dissemination).

## What this page does not cover

Page notes added with the **Notes** button are not part of the report builder as far as the source shows. The **Custom** chart group is a placeholder. Where DataSuite saves downloaded files depends on your browser or desktop settings, which the report code does not control.
